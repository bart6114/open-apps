# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Open Apps harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of identity, background, experience, appearance, ability, or
personal characteristics.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect, constructive feedback,
accountability, and a focus on what is best for the community. Unacceptable
behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or attention, trolling,
personal attacks, publishing private information, and other conduct that would
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that do
not align with these standards. Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly,
and with respect for the reporter's privacy. Do not use a public issue for a
sensitive conduct report; contact an active maintainer privately through the
address in repository commit metadata.

Consequences may include a correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent
ban depending on the impact and pattern of behavior.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is officially
representing the project.
